What to look for

  • Match the pieces to the hands. Toddlers do best with a few thick pieces they can grab by a knob or peg. Preschoolers can move on to 20-piece wooden jigsaws. Move to 100 pieces only when a child is happy with 20.
  • A picture under the piece helps. Several of the wooden sets print a full-color matching picture under each piece, so a young child can see where it goes.
  • Wood or cardboard. Chunky wooden puzzles survive being dropped and chewed on. Cardboard jigsaws are a natural next step once a child no longer mouths or throws the pieces.
  • One puzzle or a set. Multi-packs such as the dinosaur and GRINNNIE sets give you several puzzles of the same difficulty, which is useful for classrooms and for siblings.
  • Check the age on the listing. Small pieces and age grades differ from set to set, so read the age line on the Amazon page before you buy for a child under 3.

What to skip

  • Too many pieces too soon. A puzzle that is too hard ends up in the closet. Start with fewer pieces than you think.
  • Small-piece puzzles for children under 3. Stick to chunky pegs and knobs, and check the age line on the listing.
  • Adult puzzles in a kids’ chart. Our Puzzles category also lists 500 to 1,500 piece adult jigsaws, puzzle glue and storage mats. They are not kids’ puzzles.

Frequently asked questions

What puzzle is good for a 2-year-old?

Start with a chunky peg puzzle that has a few thick pieces, such as the Melissa & Doug Farm Animals or First Shapes puzzle. Check the age line on the listing.

How many pieces should a child’s puzzle have?

As a rule of thumb: a few pieces for toddlers, around 20 for a 3 to 5 year old, and about 100 for a 6 to 8 year old who likes jigsaws.

Is Kanoodle good for kids?

It is a single-player logic game that is sold for kids and adults alike. The listing counts 228 puzzles from beginner to expert, and it is our most-reviewed puzzle at 4.7★.

Wooden or cardboard puzzles?

Wooden pieces are sturdier for toddlers and preschoolers. Cardboard jigsaws are a natural next step at around 6.

The bottom line

For a toddler, buy a Melissa & Doug chunky or knob puzzle. For a preschooler, the Minnie Mouse dress-up puzzle or the Pattern Blocks set, or the dinosaur 4-pack for several puzzles in one box.
