How to choose

  • Check the age on the box. Figures with small accessories, such as swappable hands or weapons, are a choking risk for children under 3.
  • Count the joints. Preschool figures move at the head, arms and legs. Figures for older children and collectors have 20 to 30 points of articulation, which look better on a shelf but break more easily in rough play.
  • Buy the characters they already know. A multipack of heroes and villains from one show gives a child a ready-made story.

The bottom line

For a toddler, buy the Little People Story Duos or the Bluey Family 4-Pack. For ages 3 to 5, the Spidey Friends & Foes Pack or Buzz Lightyear. For ages 6 to 8, Optimus Prime.
