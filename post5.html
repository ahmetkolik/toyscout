How to choose

For stocking stuffers and party favors, stick under $10 and buy in multiples. For a standalone birthday gift, the $15–25 range buys enough volume or durability to feel like a real present rather than an add-on. Either way, check the age range on the package — markers and modeling compound skew younger than clay or paint kits.
