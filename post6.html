How to choose

Match the game to the room, not just the age on the box: quick pattern games like Taco Cat Goat Cheese Pizza work for mixed ages and short attention spans, while Flip 7 and UNO reward a few more rounds in one sitting. If reading isn't reliable yet, stick with color- and shape-matching like Candy Land until phonics catch up.

The bottom line
