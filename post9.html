How to choose

Work backwards from what you're building. A balloon arch or garland needs one mixed-size pack (the white 140-pc or gold 129-pc above) plus a color pack to accent it. Table and ceiling decor only needs a single-size 100-pack — the rainbow or pastel picks. Party favors are a separate line item entirely, and glow sticks beat balloons there because they survive the ride home. One practical note: buy roughly 20% more balloons than your plan calls for. Some will pop during inflation, and running out mid-arch means a second order and a second shipping wait.
