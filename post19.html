What to skip

  • Low-rated butter and dumpling squishies. Several slow-rise butter and dumpling squishies in the chart sit at 4.0★ or lower, well below the picks above. Some listings also mention a scent that needs a day or two to fade.
  • Squishies for under-3s. All of these are labelled 3 and up. Mini sizes are a choking risk, and toddlers bite.
  • Listings that look like NeeDoh but are not. If you want the real thing, check that the seller and brand say Schylling.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between NeeDoh and Slimygloop?

NeeDoh is filled with a thick, dough-like compound and feels heavy and solid. Slimygloop Gelli Gels are filled with gel and glitter, feel lighter and come in character shapes.

Which NeeDoh is the best?

In our catalog the Gumdrop has the best rating (4.4★) and the Nice Cube has the most ratings among the shaped NeeDohs. Either is a good first NeeDoh.

Is NeeDoh safe for toddlers?

NeeDoh is labelled for ages 3 and up. The small sizes, such as Nice Ice Baby and the advent calendar minis, are a choking risk for younger children.

What can I buy if NeeDoh is sold out?

The Big Block Squeez’M Cube is the closest feel, only bigger and firmer. Slimygloop Gelli Gels are a good choice for a child who likes characters.

The bottom line

For a first NeeDoh, buy the Gumdrop or the Nice Cube. If those are sold out, or the child squeezes hard, buy the Big Block Squeez’M Cube. For a character fan, a Slimygloop Gelli Gel.
