Best party favors for kids under $20 in 2026

Party Prep · Aug 7, 2026 · By ToyScout Editors

The goodie bag is the last thing you buy and the first thing that blows the party budget — twelve guests, five dollars a bag, and suddenly the favors cost more than the cake. The fix is bulk: every pick below is a multi-pack, all seven together come to about $74 for roughly 600 pieces, and each one holds 4.3★ or better. This is the per-child maths, not the pretty-photo list.

Start with the cost-per-child maths

A favor bag needs three or four small things in it, not one. At $12.99 for 150 pieces, the pick below lands at roughly nine cents an item — meaning a four-item bag costs about 35¢. Every single-item favor you have ever bought at a party store cost more than that whole bag.

What to skip

Pre-filled goodie bags. They cost two to three times the per-item price above and the contents are the cheapest possible filler — you are paying for the assembly. Anything with small loose parts for under-3s, which rules out most bulk fidget assortments at a toddler party. And candy as the main event: half the parents will bin it before the car ride home.

The bottom line

For a party of twelve, the honest minimum is one 30-pack of squishies and one 100-pack of glow sticks — $17 total, four items a bag, nothing left over. Scale up to the 150-piece assortment only if you are also stocking a classroom treasure box. Figures above were verified against Amazon on August 7, 2026.
