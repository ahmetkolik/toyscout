How to choose an advent calendar

  • Go by the age on the box. Most toy calendars hold small parts. For under-3s, a book calendar is the safe choice.
  • Count what is really inside. Some calendars put a toy behind every door, others mix toys with stickers or accessories. The Hot Wheels calendar, for example, has 10 cars and 14 accessories.
  • Think about what happens on December 25. LEGO builds and Hot Wheels cars join the rest of the collection, the gemstone calendar becomes jewelry, and the books stay on the shelf. The Clue calendar is played once.
  • Buy early. Popular 2026 calendars can sell out before December, so order in October or November.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best advent calendar for kids in 2026?

For ages 6 and up, the LEGO Star Wars Advent Calendar 2026 has the best rating on this list. For ages 5 and up, the LEGO City calendar. For toddlers, the Sesame Street or Bluey book calendars.

What advent calendar is good for a 3-year-old?

A book calendar such as Sesame Street or Bluey. Most toy calendars contain small parts that are not suitable under age 3.

When should you start an advent calendar?

On December 1, with door 24 opened on Christmas Eve. Buy it by November so it arrives in time.

Is there an advent calendar for the whole family?

Yes. The Clue Advent Calendar is a 24-day mystery for ages 10 and up that a family can solve together.
