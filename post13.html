Best building toys for girls in 2026, by age

Gift Guides · Sep 30, 2026 · By ToyScout Editors

You don’t need a special “girls” edition. The best-rated building sets on our chart are sold for girls and boys alike, and the right pick depends on age and theme, not colour. Below are nine picks from our Building Toys chart, grouped by age, all rated 4.6★ or better. Prices and ratings are from our September 24, 2026 catalog check.

What to skip

A “girls” edition that costs more than the standard box for the same piece count. Compare the piece count, not the colour. Sets with small pieces bought below the age on the box: a 9+ set given to a five-year-old ends up in the junk drawer. And brand-new listings with a few dozen reviews, unless the theme is the whole point of the gift.

The bottom line

Ages 3–5: PicassoTiles 100, or microMAGS for a smaller starter set. Ages 7–8: any Creator 3-in-1 animal. Ages 9–12: Botanicals. For the general, not girl-specific, list, see our best building toy gifts guide, or browse the whole Building Toys chart.
