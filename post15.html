What to skip

Surprise-egg and blind-bag toys at this price. Most of the cost goes on packaging, and repeats are common. Anything with a two-digit review count at this time of year: brand-new listings have not had time to show how they hold up. And generic “gift sets” that bundle stationery. They look generous in the listing photo, but they are mostly filler.

The bottom line

For a gift swap with one price for every child, the safe picks are SKYJO (6+) and Bananagrams (8+). For toddlers, the Mega Bloks bag has more reviews than anything else we track.
