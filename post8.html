Which kind of squishy is it?

Most disappointment with squishy toys comes from buying the wrong type. There are four main kinds:

  • Dough-filled (NeeDoh). A stretchy skin around a thick, dough-like filling. Heavy in the hand, slow to squish and fully back in shape in a second. The filling is the point, so a bite or a sharp nail can end it.
  • Gel with glitter (Slimygloop Gelli Gels). A clear skin around gel with glitter that drifts as you squeeze. More visual, a little lighter, and usually shaped like a character.
  • Slow-rise foam (butter, dumpling and mochi squishies). Soft foam that you crush flat and then watch rise back over a few seconds. Light and quiet, but the foam can tear if it is stretched instead of pressed.
  • Sticky (Crayola Globbles). Small balls that stick to walls and to each other, then wash clean. More of a throwing toy than a hand fidget.

Squishy toys by age

  • Under 3: skip squishies. Every one on this list is labelled 3 and up, the mini ones are a choking risk, and a toddler will bite through the skin. For a baby who likes to fidget, the LiKee suction-cup spinner toys are made for that age.
  • Ages 3 to 5: one larger squishy rather than minis. The NeeDoh Gumdrop or Nice Cube, or the Squishy Carrot.
  • Ages 6 to 8: character squishies such as the Gelli Gels Creeper, Crayola Globbles, or a small mochi pack to share.
  • Ages 9 and up and teens: the firmer Big Block cube, NeeDoh for school, and slow-rise butter or dumpling squishies, which are mostly bought by older kids and adults.

How to make a squishy last

  • Press, do not pull hard. Foam squishies tear when stretched. Dough and gel ones last longer but can split at the seam.
  • Keep them out of heat and sun. Gel and dough fillings soften in a hot car, and the Squeez’M listing warns that hot gel can burn.
  • Wash with soap and water.
  • Throw it away if it leaks. Do not let a young child play with a split squishy.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best squishy toy for kids?

For one child, the NeeDoh Gumdrop or Nice Cube. For a group, a pack of mini mochi squishies.

Are squishy toys safe for toddlers?

Most are labelled for ages 3 and up. Mini squishies are a choking risk, and toddlers can bite through the skin, so they are not a good toy for under-3s.

Why is NeeDoh so popular?

The dough-like filling feels soft when you squeeze slowly and firm when you squeeze fast, and it always comes back to shape. That is hard to stop playing with, for children and adults.

What is a slow-rise squishy?

A foam squishy that you squeeze flat and then watch rise back slowly. Butter, dumpling and mochi squishies are usually slow-rise.

For one good squishy, buy the NeeDoh Gumdrop. For an older child who squeezes hard, the Big Block Squeez’M Cube. For party bags, the JOYIN 200-pack.
