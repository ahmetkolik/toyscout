How to choose

For party favors and classroom giveaways, buy in bulk — the 30-pack and 160-piece assortments bring the per-unit cost down to a dollar or less. For a single gift or a sensory tool a kid will actually use daily, the NeeDoh line and the butter squishy hold up better to repeated, heavy squeezing than most bulk-pack fillers.

The bottom line

Squishies and fidgets are inexpensive enough to try a few and see what actually gets used — and unlike most toy categories, the ratings here move around a lot, so check the current score before you buy.
