Best back-to-school art supplies under $15 in 2026

Back to School · Jul 31, 2026 · By ToyScout Editors

The classroom supply list is the one shopping trip where brand loyalty actually pays off — the cheap markers dry out by October and the bargain scissors never cut construction paper. Every pick below is under $15, every one holds 4.7★ or better, and between them they carry more than 160,000 Amazon ratings. These are the supplies parents and teachers keep re-ordering year after year.

What to skip

Character-branded supply kits. They cost two to three times the component price for markers and pencils that reviewers consistently rate a full star lower. The licensing is the product. All-in-one supply boxes have the same problem — you pay for twelve items to get the four you needed, and the filler ends up in a drawer.

The bottom line

A complete, genuinely good supply kit from this list — twistables, both pencil sets, markers, scissors and construction paper — comes to about $40. That is less than most pre-assembled character kits, and every item in it is one that teachers re-order on purpose. Buy the boring brands; they are boring because they work.
