Best baby shower gifts under $25 in 2026

Gift Guides · Aug 1, 2026 · By ToyScout Editors

The baby registry is long, expensive and mostly furniture — which is exactly why the small gift is the one that gets used. Every pick below is under $25, none of them needs batteries to be worth owning, and between them they carry more than 175,000 Amazon ratings. These are the baby toys that survive the first year instead of the ones that end up in a donation box by month three.

If you only buy one thing, buy this

1. Sassy Stacks of Circles Stacking Ring. Five graduated rings, each a different texture, on a soft base that wobbles instead of tipping. At 65,000+ ratings it is the single most-reviewed baby toy on our radar, and the reason is dull and convincing: it works from about six months, when a baby can only knock the rings off, right through the second year when they can actually stack them back in order.

What to skip

Newborn toy bundles. The ten-piece gift baskets pad the count with teethers and cloth books that duplicate what four other guests are bringing, and the per-item quality drops a full star against the products above. Anything with a screen or an app — the reviews turn on battery doors and firmware, not play. And 0-3 month clothing, the classic shower mistake: babies outgrow it in weeks and every registry already has too much.

The bottom line

All seven items together come to about $65 — less than a single mid-range registry item, and enough to cover a baby from the newborn rattle stage through the first birthday. If you are buying one gift, make it the Sassy stacking rings; if the baby has not arrived yet, make it the Oball rattle. Figures above were verified against Amazon on August 1, 2026.
