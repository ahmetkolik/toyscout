How to choose

Magnetic tiles (PicassoTiles, MAGNA-TILES) suit kids who want to see a shape stand up immediately — good for shorter attention spans and ages 3+. LEGO brick sets reward kids who already sit through longer, more detailed builds. If you're shopping early for the holidays, buy the exact style and color now — restocks on the popular sets get unpredictable from November on.
