Which Play-Doh pack should you buy? Every size compared by price per ounce

Buying Guides · Sep 30, 2026 · By ToyScout Editors

Play-Doh sells the same compound in pack sizes from 1-ounce sticks to 4-ounce cans, and depending on the pack you pay anywhere from 27¢ to 40¢ an ounce. We took the six Play-Doh packs on our Arts & Crafts chart and did the maths. All six are rated 4.7★ or better. Prices are from our September 24, 2026 catalog check.

The quick answer

  • Cheapest per ounce: the 36-pack of 3-oz cans, about 27¢/oz.
  • Best value without buying six pounds: the 12-pack of 4-oz cans, about 28¢/oz.
  • Best first pack or refill: the 10-pack of 2-oz cans. It costs the most per ounce (40¢) but has the lowest total price.
  • For handing out: the 42-pack of 1-oz cans or the 30 individually wrapped Halloween sticks.

1. The classic 10-pack: where most families start

Ten 2-ounce cans in red, orange, yellow, green, teal, blue, purple, pink, black and white for $7.99. This is the best-selling pack: #1 in Kids’ Art Clay & Dough, #17 across all of Toys & Games, with 69,322 ratings. At 40¢ an ounce it is the most expensive per ounce, but at under $8 it is the obvious first purchase or refill for a playset. The listing notes that it contains wheat. See the 10-pack on ToyScout.

Thirty-six 3-ounce cans, over six pounds of compound, for $28.99, or about 27¢ an ounce. That is a third cheaper per ounce than the 10-pack. It makes sense for classrooms, childminders and houses with more than one child. For a single child, six pounds is a lot of compound to store.

What to skip

Small cans for play at home. The 1-ounce cans cost around 40% more per ounce than the 36-pack for the same compound, and that premium only pays off when you are giving the cans away. A playset as the only purchase. Playsets usually come with only a few cans, so if you want more compound to play with, a pack of cans is the cheaper way to get it.

The bottom line

One child: buy the 12-pack of 4-oz cans. Classroom or several children: the 36-pack. First time or topping up a playset: the 10-pack. Party bags or Halloween: the 42-pack or the Spooky sticks.
