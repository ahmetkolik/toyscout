Which Play-Doh pack should you buy? Every size compared

Buying Guides · Sep 30, 2026 · By ToyScout Editors

Play-Doh sells the same compound in packs from 1-ounce sticks to 4-ounce cans. The right pack depends on how many children are playing, whether you are handing cans out, and how much colour variety you want. We compared the six Play-Doh packs on our Arts & Crafts chart, all rated 4.7★ or better. Ratings and ranks are from our September 24, 2026 catalog check.

The quick answer

  • Most compound in one box: the 36-pack of 3-oz cans, 108 ounces in total.
  • One child at home: the 12-pack of 4-oz cans, the biggest cans Play-Doh sells in a multi-pack.
  • First pack or playset refill: the classic 10-pack of 2-oz cans.
  • For handing out: the 42-pack of 1-oz cans or the 30 individually wrapped Halloween sticks.
  • Most colours: the 65-pack, with 60 different colours.

1. The classic 10-pack: where most families start

Ten 2-ounce cans in red, orange, yellow, green, teal, blue, purple, pink, black and white. This is the best-selling pack: #1 in Kids’ Art Clay & Dough, #17 across all of Toys & Games, with 69,322 ratings. Ten colours is enough for a first set, and it is the usual refill for a playset. Thirty-six 3-ounce cans, 108 ounces or over six pounds of compound. It is the pack for classrooms, childminders and houses with more than one child, where every child can have their own colours. For a single child, six pounds is a lot of compound to store.

3. The 12-pack of 4-oz cans: the biggest cans

Twelve 4-ounce cans, 48 ounces in total, which is more than double the classic 10-pack. Big cans mean fewer lids to open and close, and enough of each colour for larger builds. This version comes in winter colours (forest green, red, white and blues), and it ranks #2 in its category. For most single-child households, this is the pack to buy.

What to skip

Small cans for play at home. A 1-ounce can holds a quarter of a 4-ounce can, so you open and reseal four times as many lids for the same amount of compound. Small cans earn their place when you are giving them away. A playset as the only purchase. Playsets usually come with only a few cans, so if you want more compound to play with, add a pack of cans.

The bottom line

One child: buy the 12-pack of 4-oz cans. Classroom or several children: the 36-pack. First time or topping up a playset: the 10-pack. Party bags or Halloween: the 42-pack or the Spooky sticks.
